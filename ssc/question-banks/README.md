Review artifacts are retained with each batch. The metadata-hidden packets are not independent blind audits; all review limitations are recorded in the individual batch notes.

The full cross-lesson review of all 672 items is documented in [full_remaining_review.md](full_remaining_review.md).

All lesson banks now include item-level, provisional Bloom classifications for MCQs and structured responses. See each bank’s `bloom_review_full.json` for rationales and distributions; classifications are same-session AI reviews and are not independently validated.

MCQ explanations were revised to state the reasoning directly; the shared item-level edits are in [explanation_overrides.py](explanation_overrides.py), and the scope is recorded in [full_remaining_review.md](full_remaining_review.md).

Every MCQ now has feedback attached to all four answer choices, including a specific explanation for each distractor. The shared authored feedback and option-order checks are in [option_feedback_overrides.py](option_feedback_overrides.py). The quiz reveals all choice explanations after an answer is submitted.
