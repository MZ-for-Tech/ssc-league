Given this compound-interest program, identify its inputs and explain what the loop prints at the end of year 1. State the final compound interest and explain the role of `**` in the calculation.

P = 10000
R = 12
T = 1
n = 4
year = 1

    A = P * (1 + R / (100 * n)) ** (n * year)
    print("End of year", year, "-> A =", round(A, 2), "EGP")
    year = year + 1
CI = A - P
print("Compound interest =", round(CI, 2), "EGP")
