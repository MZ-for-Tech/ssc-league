# Structured-response review

All eight prompts include an expected answer, an explicit answer justification, and a five-point rubric. Tuple unpacking, dictionary operations, survey and marketing results, normalized character counts, and the signed elasticity calculations were checked. The last essay asks students to identify a source-code classification problem directly.

The review is same-session and is not independent instructor validation. Source caveats are listed in `source_review.md`.
