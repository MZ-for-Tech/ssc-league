# Stimulus and task variety

The bank includes 14 distinct SVG figures: 12 produced through pandas plotting calls and two through Seaborn. They cover grouped and horizontal bars, histograms, scatter plots, box plots, a line chart, pie charts, and a 100%-stacked bar. Chart stimuli are distributed across the bank, with descriptive alt text; all plotted values are illustrative. The questions test chart reading, chart selection, coding, and interpretation rather than repeating one graphic throughout. The exact renderer for each image is listed in `charts/manifest.json`.
