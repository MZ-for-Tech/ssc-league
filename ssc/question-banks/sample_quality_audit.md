# Sample quality and source-alignment audit

## Scope

Reviewed one MCQ and one essay from each of the 14 lesson banks: the item numbered `016` and the essay numbered `E04` in each bank (28 items total). For each item, checked the stem, answer key or expected response, explanation/justification, rubric, and cited lesson source. This is a coverage sample, not an independent audit of all 672 items.

## Findings

- In the reviewed sample, the keyed MCQ answers and their explanations matched the cited lesson material. The essay expected answers, justifications, and scoring criteria also aligned with the sampled lesson content.
- MCQ `M2L5-016` had two distractors that expressed the same behavior as the correct answer (`sorted(x)` returns a new sorted list and leaves `x` unchanged). Replaced the duplicate with a distinct misconception: “It sorts x in place and returns a separate sorted list.” The answer key remains the fourth option.
- Updated the canonical item, the metadata-hidden MCQ packet, the question preview, and the generator's option definition so the fix persists.

## Limits

This sample checks source alignment and item construction for 28 items. It does not establish that every unsampled item is error-free, and it is not an independent instructor review.
