# Third sample quality and source-alignment audit

## Scope

Reviewed a fresh set of 28 items, avoiding the first two samples: MCQ `024` and essay `E02` from each of the 14 lesson banks. Checked stems, keyed/expected answers, explanations/justifications, rubrics, and cited source content. This is a coverage sample, not a review of all 672 questions.

## Findings

- The sampled answer keys, explanations, essay answers, justifications, and rubrics aligned with their cited lesson content. No answer-content mismatches were found in this sample.
- Three Bloom labels overstated the cognitive demand: `M2L1-024` was a direct recall of a stated convention rather than Evaluate; `M2L1-E02` primarily traces familiar augmented assignments rather than Analyze; and `M2L3-E02` applies `range()` endpoint rules rather than Analyze. Updated these to Remember, Apply, and Apply, respectively, in the item data and Bloom reports.

## Limits

This sample checks source alignment and item construction for 28 items. It does not establish that every unsampled item is error-free, and it is not an independent instructor review.
